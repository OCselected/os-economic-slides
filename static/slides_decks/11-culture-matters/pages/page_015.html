Open Source Definition — 十条准则
钟已敲响，开源的召唤自1998年延续至今
1
自由再分发 Free redistribution
2
源代码提供 Source code provided
3
允许衍生作品 Derivative works permitted
4
作者源代码完整性 Author's source code integrity
5
不歧视个人或群体 No discrimination against persons or groups
6
不歧视任何领域 No discrimination against fields of endeavor
7
分发时附带许可 License included with distribution
8
许可不得针对特定产品 License not specific to a product
9
许可不得限制其他软件 License may not restrict other software
10
许可必须技术中立 License must be technology-neutral
